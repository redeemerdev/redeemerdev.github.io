Features

Everything in My Memory Verse

Named and described the way they appear in the app.

Community Feed

Discover Bible verses one at a time. Swipe through the Community Feed, heart verses you want to memorize, and keep reading.

Your Faves Collection

Access all your favorite verses here. Tap the heart to remove a verse from this list. Long press anywhere to reveal the verse reference. Release to hide it again.

Practice Session

Strengthen your Scripture memory through guided recall. Practice questions draw only from verses you saved to Favorites. Choose a level — from identifying references to typing full verses.

Swipe Recall

Swipe through Bible verses to reinforce memory.

Quick Recall

Match the Bible reference with the correct verse before time or lives run out (based on the selected mode).

Levels

Level 1 – Reference, Level 2 – Verse, Level 3 – Type Reference, Level 4 – Type Verse, and Level 5 – Ultimate Recall.

Current Streak

Read 10 verses in a single day from Community Feed or Favorites to complete a day and maintain your streak.

Add Verse

Add Custom Verses so your personal collection is ready for reading and Practice Session.

Achievements

Track your progress as you memorize God's Word — across Consistency, Reading, Favorites, Memorization, Practice, and Community.

Memorization

Long press anywhere to reveal the verse reference. Release to hide it again. Keep returning to verses until Scripture stays with you.

How It Works

Community Feed → Favorites → Practice Session

Discover verses in the Community Feed, tap the heart to save them to Favorites, then practice here until you tap End Practice Session. Build a daily habit of Scripture memorization — choose Swipe Recall, Quick Recall, or Levels 1–5 and strengthen your memory one verse at a time.
