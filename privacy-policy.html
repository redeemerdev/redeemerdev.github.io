Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how My Memory Verse, an Android application published by Redeemer Dev, collects, uses, and retains information. It describes the current application, version 3.0.0. Last updated November 22, 2026.

We respect your privacy.

My Memory Verse uses information to sign you in, save and sync your verses, support the community and public leaderboards, and understand app usage. The app does not show advertisements and does not sell a subscription.

Information We Use

These practices apply to My Memory Verse on Android, version 3.0.0.

Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication

If you choose to sign in with Google, authentication is handled by Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication. My Memory Verse does not create or store a password for your account.

When you sign in, the app may receive and store:

  • Your Google account name
  • Your Google account email address
  • Your Google account user ID, a unique identifier provided by Google

That information is used to create and recognize your My Memory Verse account, associate your cloud data with you, and respond to account requests such as deletion.

Username and avatar ID

If you create a community profile, My Memory Verse stores the username you choose and an avatar ID for the avatar you select. Your username can be shown with community verses and on public leaderboards. Your avatar ID can be shown with community verses.

Usernames are public. Please keep them respectful and appropriate for a Christian community, as described in the Community Guidelines.

Community verses and custom Bible verses

When you share a community verse, Cloud Firestore may store the verse text, reference, translation information, your username, avatar ID, the time of the post, and community interactions such as hearts.

Custom Bible verses you add are stored so you can read and practice them. Depending on the feature you use, a custom verse may remain on your device, be included in cloud synchronization, or be shared by you to the community.

Favorites, streaks, practice statistics, and cloud synchronization

Favorites, memorization streaks, and practice statistics may be stored on your device. When you are signed in, My Memory Verse can synchronize Favorites, custom Bible verses, and memorization streaks to Cloud Firestore so they can be restored with your account. Practice statistics are included in that cloud synchronization when you are signed in.

Cloud synchronization may include:

  • Favorites
  • Custom Bible verses saved to your account
  • Memorization streaks, including the current streak, best streak, and last read date
  • Practice statistics, when you are signed in

Signing out does not delete this information. Uninstalling the app does not delete cloud data associated with your account.

Public leaderboards

My Memory Verse offers public weekly and all-time leaderboards for qualifying practice results. Submitting a score requires Google Sign-In. It does not require a subscription. Other users of that feature may see your community username, score, and a completion time when the mode records one. The entry is stored with your account user ID. Avatar IDs appear with community verses; they are not a separate leaderboard field.

Reports and device-related identifiers

If you report a community verse or a username, My Memory Verse may store the details needed to review the report. Report records are not shown publicly in the community.

A report may include:

  • The reported post or content identifier
  • The verse reference or reported username
  • The reason you select
  • Your account identifier as the reporter
  • A device-related identifier collected through the report
  • The time of the report

Device-related identifiers collected through reports are used to review abuse, prevent spam, and protect the community. They are not used to show advertisements.

If you acknowledge the Community Guidelines and Terms of Service in the app, your account profile may store the accepted version and the time of that acknowledgment.

Local device-level blocking

You can block another user on your device. A block is stored locally on that device. It is used to hide that user’s community content for you on that device.

A local block is not a public list, and it does not by itself delete the other person’s account, community verses, or leaderboard entry for everyone else.

Firebase Analytics

My Memory Verse uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and to improve reliability and future updates. Analytics may process usage events together with general technical information, such as an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, and language.

Analytics information is handled through Google Firebase. It is not used to display advertisements in My Memory Verse.

Advertising

My Memory Verse does not display advertisements. The app does not use Google AdMob and does not offer rewarded advertisements.

No in-app purchases

Version 3.0.0 does not sell a subscription or any other in-app purchase. The app does not use Google Play Billing and does not offer Restore Purchases.

If you subscribed in an earlier version, Google Play may still renew that subscription until you cancel it in Google Play. Redeemer Dev does not receive or store your payment card number or full billing details. Deleting your My Memory Verse account does not cancel an earlier subscription. See the terms for earlier subscriptions.

How we use information

Information described in this policy may be used to:

  • Provide reading, practice, favorites, and custom-verse features
  • Authenticate your account with Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication
  • Store and synchronize account data in Cloud Firestore
  • Show community verses and public leaderboards
  • Review reports and support local blocking
  • Maintain memorization streaks and practice statistics
  • Understand app usage through Firebase Analytics
  • Protect the service from abuse and spam
  • Respond to support and account-deletion requests

Who receives information

My Memory Verse uses these Google services to operate the app: Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Analytics. Version 3.0.0 does not use Google Play Billing. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies, including Google’s Privacy Policy.

Other users can see information you place in public parts of the app, including community verses and public leaderboard entries. Reports are used for review and are not displayed as public community posts.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertising networks for My Memory Verse, because the app does not show advertisements.

Data retention

Account information, cloud-synchronized favorites, custom verses, memorization streaks, and practice statistics are kept while your account exists so the app can provide those features.

After a verified deletion request, associated Firebase records are manually reviewed and deleted. Shared community verses may require a separate administrative action and may be anonymized rather than deleted, so the verse can remain available without your username.

Information stored only on your device, including a local block list, stays on that device until you clear app storage or remove it. Uninstalling the app does not delete cloud data.

Report records are kept as needed to review the report and protect the community. Firebase Analytics information is retained according to Firebase retention settings. If you purchased a subscription in an earlier version, Google may keep those Play Billing records under Google’s own policies after an account is deleted. Emails you send us are kept as needed to verify the request, complete deletion, and keep a record of the support request.

Account deletion

Signed-in users can request account deletion from inside the app. The app opens the Delete Account page. You can also email mymemoryverseapp@gmail.com and include the Google account email associated with My Memory Verse.

Opening that page does not by itself delete the account. The developer manually verifies the request and then manually reviews and deletes associated Firebase records. Requests are processed within a reasonable period. If you subscribed in an earlier version, deleting an account does not cancel that Google Play subscription.

The full process is explained on the Delete Account page.

Your choices

  • Sign in with Google only if you choose to use features that require an account.
  • Choose whether to share a community verse. Submitting a public leaderboard score requires Google Sign-In and does not require a subscription.
  • Block another user locally on your device.
  • If you subscribed in an earlier version, cancel that subscription in Google Play if you do not want it to renew.
  • Request account deletion from inside the app, or by email, as described on the Delete Account page.
  • Clear information stored only on your device through your device settings.

Uninstalling My Memory Verse removes the local app, but it does not delete cloud data. If you subscribed in an earlier version, uninstalling does not cancel that Google Play subscription.

Children

My Memory Verse is a general-audience Scripture memorization app. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will review the request.

Security and international processing

We use the access controls provided by Firebase and Google to protect account data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Google Firebase may process information on servers outside the country where you live. That infrastructure is used to provide authentication, cloud storage, and analytics. Google Play may still process an earlier subscription under Google’s own terms.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. This version describes Android application version 3.0.0.

This website

The My Memory Verse pages on this website are static information pages. They do not require an account. If you email us, we use the message and your email address to respond and, when you ask, to process account deletion.
