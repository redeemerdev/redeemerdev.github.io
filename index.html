About Redeemer Dev

Redeemer Dev is an independent app development project focused on creating simple and meaningful digital tools for everyday Christian life.

My goal is to build apps that help people engage more deeply with God's Word—whether through Scripture memorization, Bible study, or other practical tools that support their faith.

I believe technology can be simple, useful, and purposeful.
